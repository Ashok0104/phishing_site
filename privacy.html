Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as email addresses for newsletters or URLs submitted for analysis. We also collect usage data to improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to provide and improve our phishing detection services, respond to inquiries, and send updates if you subscribe to our newsletter.

3. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. URLs submitted for analysis are not stored unless explicitly saved in reports.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or to protect our services.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@safebankshield.com to exercise these rights.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Effective Date: April 24, 2025

If you have questions, please contact us.
