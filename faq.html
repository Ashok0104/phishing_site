Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SafeBank Shield and phishing protection.

What is SafeBank Shield?

SafeBank Shield is an advanced phishing detection tool that uses associative classification to protect users from e-banking phishing attacks.

How does the detection tool work?

Our tool analyzes URLs, content, SSL certificates, and other factors to determine if a website is safe, using real-time machine learning algorithms.

Is my data safe with SafeBank Shield?

Yes, we prioritize user privacy and do not store sensitive information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use SafeBank Shield on mobile?

Absolutely! Our tool is fully responsive and works on all devices, including smartphones and tablets.
